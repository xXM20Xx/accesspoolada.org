Ticker AXESS, pronounced “access”

Removing barriers, funded by staking

AXESS is a Cardano stake pool. Half of the operator's margin goes to charitable nonprofits that remove barriers for people, in areas like clean water, disability access, technology, and hearing. Delegators vote on which ones.

Direct dollars for direct services.

Status: running on the Cardano Preview test network. Not yet accepting mainnet delegation.

How the money works

Each epoch, the operator takes a fixed fee and a margin from the pool's rewards, and delegators share the rest. Donations come out of the operator's share, never out of delegators' rewards.

Fixed fee
170 ADA per epoch, the network minimum, until the pool passes 10 million ADA delegated. Then 340 ADA. A flat fee takes a bigger share of rewards when a pool is small, so it starts at the minimum.
Margin
5% of the rewards left after the fixed fee, from the start.
Donated
50% of the operator margin. With a 5% margin, that is 2.5% of the pool's rewards after the fixed fee.

Any pool can change its fee and margin at any time. This schedule was published before the pool had any delegators, and any change will be announced here at least two epochs before it takes effect. The fee only goes up once delegated stake actually passes 10 million ADA. Stake figures are public, so you can check.

Who qualifies

Registered charitable nonprofits (501(c)(3) in the US, or the local equivalent elsewhere) whose main work is direct services that remove barriers for people.

Never eligible: political candidates, campaigns, parties or officeholders; PACs; lobbying organizations and groups whose main activity is political advocacy; ballot-measure committees.

How recipients are chosen

  1. A shortlist of vetted organizations is published here, with the criteria used to pick them.
  2. Delegators vote, weighted by stake.
  3. The results and the amount sent are published here.

Voting happens once a year at first, then quarterly once the amounts are big enough. If too few delegators vote, the money goes to a default recipient.

Receipts

Every donation is recorded on-chain as transaction metadata tied to this pool's ID, so you can check it yourself.

About this site

This site is built to work with a screen reader and a keyboard, and to resize without breaking. It follows your system's light or dark setting, and color is never the only way information is shown. It uses no JavaScript, no tracking, and no cookies.

If something here is hard to use, please report it.

Who runs it

AXESS runs on a small, low-power computer at home. That keeps costs down and helps keep the network spread across many independent operators.

AXESS is a stake pool that donates. It is not a registered charity, and contributions to it are not tax-deductible. The .org domain reflects the mission, not a legal status.
